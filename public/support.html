Support & community guidelines

Need help? Email glossortossapp@gmail.com. Include the product name or the problem you experienced. Never send your password or verification code.

Account help

Use Forgot password on Sign In to receive a reset email. You can permanently delete your account from Profile → Account settings → Delete my account.

Keep it honest and helpful

Vote from your own experience. No spam, harassment, hateful or sexual content, impersonation, personal information about others, or manipulated votes. Brands cannot buy positive verdicts.

Photos and submissions

Submit only product photos you took and own, with the required photo permission. No screenshots or downloaded website images. All submissions are reviewed before publication. We can reject submissions, remove content, and suspend abusive accounts.

Report a problem

Open a product’s Details and choose Report content. For copyright concerns or if you cannot sign in, email us with the product link, description of the concern, and your contact details. Reports are reviewed by the site administrator.
