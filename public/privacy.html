Privacy

Updated September 12, 2026. This policy covers the Gloss or Toss website and app.

Information we use

We use your email and authentication account to let you sign in, verify your email, and reset your password. We store your votes, Watchlist, product submissions, uploaded photos, photo permission records, and content reports. Technical information, including IP-derived rate-limit identifiers and service logs, helps operate and protect the service.

What other people see

Product listings, approved photos, and aggregate community scores are public. Your email, private Watchlist, and individual vote history are not shown on public profiles. Administrators can review account information and submissions to operate and moderate the service.

Service providers and links

Netlify provides hosting, authentication, database, and photo storage. Verification and password-reset messages are handled through our authentication service. Purchase and social links open third-party services with their own privacy policies. Some purchase links may earn us an affiliate commission.

Photos and sharing

Upload only photos you own and agree to license under the photo terms shown when submitting. Approved photos may appear on the site, app, share cards, social media, and Gloss or Toss advertising. We resize and process uploads for display. Downloaded copies shared by others may remain outside our control.

Storage and deletion

Account information and activity are kept while your account is active. Profile → Account settings → Delete my account removes your login, votes, Watchlist, submissions, and stored submitted photos. Shared product listings and other members’ activity remain. An irreversible hash of the deleted authentication identifier prevents old sessions from recreating the account. Service backups and cached copies may remain temporarily under the hosting provider’s retention policies.

Your choices

You can change saved votes, remove Watchlist items, and choose whether to submit a photo. Sign-in cookies maintain your session. For access, correction, privacy questions, or help, email glossortossapp@gmail.com.
